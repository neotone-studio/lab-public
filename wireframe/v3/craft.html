Why the note names change as you build

Build a D Kurd and something quiet happens. You place the note your ear calls an A sharp, and the instrument writes it down as a B flat. Nothing has changed about the pitch. What changed is the name, and the name is the instrument telling you what it thinks you are building.

What is Spatial MIDI?

Standard MIDI describes an event: this note, this hard, now. The instrument senses something continuous and multidimensional. Spatial MIDI is the attempt to carry the second thing through a channel built for the first.
