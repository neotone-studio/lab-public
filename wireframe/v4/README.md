# Neotone Wireframe v4

Built from v3. One structural change, with its consequences.

**The nav is Tonefield · Instruments.** Culture and Craft collapsed into one publication, named. The instrument section became plural, with a submenu of names.

```
              NEOTONE
      Tonefield    Instruments
```

Why, and the editorial rules that go with it, are in the content structure document in the workspace `docs/`. This file is what changed in the build. [site-structure.md](site-structure.md) is the component reference.

---

## What changed from v3

| v3 | v4 |
|---|---|
| Culture · Instrument · Craft | Tonefield · Instruments |
| `index.html` is Culture, `craft.html` is Craft | one feed at `index.html`; `craft.html` removed |
| Instrument is one link | Instruments is a group with a submenu: One, Anima, Treangle |
| Anima page with placeholder sections | Anima and Treangle as placeholders that promise nothing |
| Manual's third register marked neOS | marked Tonefield |
| neOS unnamed anywhere | named on the instrument page |
| No updates archive | `updates.html`, dated, linked from footer and sign-up |
| Short pages stopped collapsing part way | ramps scale to the page's scroll range |

**Culture and craft survive as kickers**, and as a curation rule: the first screen of Tonefield shows one of each, with the second piece's title pulled up inside the first viewport. That is how the site addresses two kinds of reader at once without a taxonomy to maintain.

**Updates carry dates; Tonefield pieces do not.** The one visible difference between the two feeds, and the species difference.

**The feed holds seven real pieces**, titles and standfirsts for work genuinely in progress, sourced from the 1000th-instrument draft, the artist interviews and the manual's third register. The landing makes its own case instead of showing grey boxes.

## Growth

| When | Nav |
|---|---|
| Now | Tonefield · Instruments (One) |
| Anima ships | Tonefield · Instruments (One, Anima) |
| neOS ships standalone | Tonefield · Instruments · Software |

No rename events. A name enters the nav when the thing ships. An ad landing page is the instrument page shown without the publication around it: not a page type, never in the nav.

## Open

- **Mobile.** The bar is undesigned below 980px. The submenu, the collapse travel and the footer bands are untested at phone width.
- **Workshop page** is still an alert. It is a utility page carrying the team and the company boilerplate.
- **Tonefield has no gloss line.** The context decodes it as a publication, and for handpan players the word already means the playing surface. Reversible if it tests badly.
- **Whether the thousandth-instrument material is one piece or two.**
- **The wordmark choreography of v2 is gone**, traded for a structure that scales. Worth knowing what was traded.
