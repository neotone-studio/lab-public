PROMPT FOR CHATGPT IMAGE GENERATION
Neotone One, "connections" image for the product page
Written 2026-08-12. Attach a photograph of the Neotone One with this prompt.

===============================================================================
READ THIS FIRST (context for whoever is running the prompt, not part of it)
===============================================================================

The image sits on a product page for a single handmade instrument. The page is
a quiet editorial layout: white background, black text, hairline grey rules,
Arial, no chrome, no shadows, no gradients, no colour anywhere.

The point of the image is NOT to show connectivity. It is to show that the
instrument does not require anything. It is a location image, not a wiring
diagram. Emptiness is the content. If the finished image looks like the back
panel diagram in an audio interface manual, it has failed.

Generate WITHOUT TEXT. Labels are added in HTML afterwards so they stay crisp,
editable and consistent with the site's typeface. Do not ask the model for
lettering, callout text, captions or a title.

Two variants are requested at the end. Generate both.

===============================================================================
PROMPT BEGINS
===============================================================================

I am attaching a photograph of a musical instrument called the Neotone One. It
is a handmade wooden handpan-shaped electronic instrument: a round body with a
domed playing surface, made of hardwood.

Create a single flat, minimal illustration for a website, using the attached
instrument as the subject.

SUBJECT AND COMPOSITION

Place the instrument alone at the exact centre of the frame, seen from directly
above, filling roughly one third of the image width. Render it faithfully to the
attached photograph: the same proportions, the same wood, the same arrangement
of playing surfaces. It should read as this specific object, not a generic
drum or a symbol of one.

Surround it with a large amount of empty white space. The emptiness is the
subject of the picture as much as the instrument is. Do not fill it.

At the outer edges of the frame, place four or five very small, very simple
outline glyphs, drawn in thin uniform grey line work, evenly distributed around
the instrument and set well back from it. Each glyph should be no more than
about one twentieth of the image width, so that they read as faint notes at the
periphery rather than as components of a system. Suggested glyphs, drawn as
minimal outlines:

  - a pair of headphones
  - a small laptop, closed or seen edge on, deliberately unremarkable
  - a simple wifi arc
  - a plain speaker cone or monitor outline
  - one small blank rounded rectangle standing in for an unspecified device

Leave meaningful empty gaps between them. Do not arrange them in a perfect ring
or a symmetrical clock face; a slightly uneven scatter reads as optional, an
even ring reads as a system diagram.

CRITICAL RULES ABOUT CONNECTIONS

  - Draw NO cables, NO wires, NO arrows, NO connecting lines of any kind
    between the instrument and the peripheral glyphs. Nothing touches the
    instrument. This is the single most important instruction in the brief.
  - The instrument must not appear to be plugged into anything.
  - Do not draw ports, sockets, jacks or connectors on the instrument.
  - Do not add a signal flow, a routing path, or any indication of direction.

The relationship between the centre and the edges must be conveyed only by
position and by size. The instrument is large, central, fully rendered and
complete. Everything else is small, distant, faint and clearly optional.

STYLE

  - Absolutely flat. No perspective on the peripheral glyphs, no isometric
    view, no 3D rendering, no photorealism outside the instrument itself.
  - No drop shadows, no glows, no reflections, no gradients, no textures, no
    background patterns, no grid, no vignette.
  - Pure white background, hex #FFFFFF, edge to edge.
  - Peripheral glyphs in a single light grey line, roughly hex #B8B8B8, with
    a consistent hairline stroke weight of about 1.5 pixels at 2000 pixels
    wide. No fills.
  - The instrument may be rendered more richly than the glyphs, either as a
    clean photographic cutout on white, or as a detailed line and tone drawing.
    It should be the only element in the image with any weight or detail.
  - Entirely monochrome apart from the natural colour of the wood. No accent
    colour anywhere. No brand colours.
  - No text, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no title, no watermark, no
    logo, no signature.
  - No people, no hands, no room, no furniture, no plants, no studio setting,
    no desk.

FORMAT

  - Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape.
  - At least 2000 pixels wide.
  - Generous internal margins: nothing should come close to the frame edge.
    Keep the outermost glyphs at least 8 percent of the image width in from
    every edge, because HTML labels will be positioned just outside them.

WHAT IT SHOULD FEEL LIKE

Someone glancing at this for one second should think: that instrument is
complete on its own, and those other things are around it if you want them. It
should feel calm, spacious and slightly austere, closer to a museum object card
than to a product manual or a gear advertisement.

WHAT IT MUST NOT LOOK LIKE

  - An audio interface connection diagram
  - A "studio setup" or signal chain illustration
  - A hub and spoke network graphic
  - An infographic
  - A product feature callout image with lines pointing at parts
  - Anything where a computer appears equal in size or importance to the
    instrument

VARIANTS

Generate two versions:

  1. As described above, with the peripheral glyphs.
  2. Identical composition and style, with the instrument alone and no
     peripheral glyphs at all: just the instrument, centred, on white, with the
     same generous margins.

===============================================================================
PROMPT ENDS
===============================================================================

NOTES FOR REVIEWING THE RESULT

Reject and regenerate if any of the following are present, since each defeats
the purpose of the image:

  - any line, cable or arrow touching or approaching the instrument
  - peripheral glyphs large enough to compete with the instrument
  - the glyphs arranged in an even ring, which reads as a system
  - a laptop drawn with any prominence, detail or screen content
  - shadows, gradients, or an off-white background
  - generated text of any kind
  - the instrument rendered generically rather than as the attached object

Variant 2, the instrument alone, is worth keeping regardless. It may turn out
to be the better image for the top of the product page, in which case the
connections stay as the existing list of rows further down and this exercise
will have settled the question rather than answered it in the expected
direction.
