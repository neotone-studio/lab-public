PROMPT FOR CHATGPT IMAGE GENERATION
Neotone One, hero photograph for the product page
Written 2026-08-12. Attach a photograph of the Neotone One with this prompt.

===============================================================================
READ THIS FIRST (context for whoever is running the prompt, not part of it)
===============================================================================

This is the first image on the product page for a single handmade instrument,
made in a workshop in Budapest. The page around it is quiet and editorial:
white background, black text, hairline rules, Arial, no chrome. The photograph
is the only place colour enters, so it carries all of the warmth.

The image has one job: show the instrument in a life, not in a rig. Someone
should look at it and understand what kind of person owns one and what kind of
room it lives in. It is a picture about belonging, and the instrument happens
to be in it.

It is NOT a gear photograph. If it looks like a home studio shot from a music
retailer, or a product on a seamless backdrop, or a desk covered in equipment,
it has failed.

Generate WITHOUT TEXT of any kind.

Two variants are requested at the end. Generate both.

===============================================================================
PROMPT BEGINS
===============================================================================

I am attaching a photograph of a musical instrument called the Neotone One. It
is a handmade wooden handpan-shaped electronic instrument: a round body with a
domed playing surface, made of hardwood. Reproduce it faithfully. Its
proportions, its wood grain, and the arrangement of its playing surfaces must
match the attached photograph exactly. It must read as this specific object.

Create a photorealistic photograph of this instrument in a real room.

THE ROOM

An older Central European apartment or workshop, the kind found in Budapest.
Specific things that make it right:

  - tall windows with slim glazing bars, daylight coming in from one side
  - worn parquet or plain wooden floorboards
  - plain plaster walls, slightly imperfect, off white or pale warm grey
  - high ceiling implied by the framing rather than shown in full
  - a few honest objects: a wooden stool or a simple chair, a rug, a stack of
    books or paper, a cup, a coat over a chair back
  - lived in but uncluttered. Someone works here. It has not been styled

Daylight only. Soft, directional, slightly cool, the light of an overcast
afternoon through a large window. No lamps, no coloured lighting, no studio
strobes, no lens flare.

THE INSTRUMENT IN THE ROOM

The instrument is present and clearly the point of the picture, but it is not
isolated or displayed. It sits on a stand, on a low table, or on a chair, at a
height where someone would sit down and play it. It should look like it was
left there by a person a moment ago.

It occupies roughly a quarter to a third of the frame. It is in sharp focus.
The room around it falls off gently, with a shallow but not extreme depth of
field, as from a 35mm or 50mm lens at a moderate aperture.

Frame it slightly off centre, with breathing room around it, so the image
reads as a photograph of a room that contains this instrument.

HUMAN PRESENCE

There is evidence of a person and no portrait. Do not put a face in the frame.
Acceptable and encouraged: a chair pulled out, a jacket over its back, an open
notebook with handwriting too small to read, worn slippers, a half drunk cup of
coffee, a window left open.

Do not show hands playing the instrument. Do not show a seated player. Do not
show anyone at all.

WHAT MUST NOT BE IN THE PICTURE

  - laptops, tablets, phones, screens of any kind
  - studio monitors, mixing desks, rack gear, cables, microphones on stands
  - other branded instruments or equipment, and any visible logos
  - acoustic foam, treatment panels, or anything that says recording studio
  - fairy lights, neon, plants arranged for effect, styled interior decor
  - a seamless backdrop, a plinth, a display case, a shop
  - text, watermarks, signatures, captions

COLOUR AND TREATMENT

Muted and natural. Warm wood against cool daylight and pale plaster. Low
saturation overall, no filter look, no teal and orange grade, no heavy
vignette, no film grain effect, no HDR. It should look like an unhurried
photograph taken by someone who knows the room, not like an advertisement.

FORMAT

  - Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape, at least 2400 pixels wide.
  - Compose so the image can also be cropped to a 2.4:1 letterbox band without
    losing the instrument or the window light.
  - Keep the instrument away from the extreme left and right edges.

WHAT IT SHOULD FEEL LIKE

Quiet, warm, slightly austere. A room in a European city on an ordinary
afternoon, with a beautiful wooden instrument in it that someone plays. A
viewer should be able to imagine living there.

VARIANTS

Generate two versions:

  1. As described above: the instrument in the room, no person, evidence of a
     person.

  2. The same room and the same light, photographed from further back, with the
     instrument smaller in the frame and more of the room visible. Same rules:
     no person, no screens, no equipment. This tests whether the situated
     reading gets stronger as the instrument gets smaller.

PROMPT ENDS

NOTES FOR REVIEWING THE RESULT

The first thing to check is fidelity. Image models distort real objects when
placing them into scenes. If the instrument's proportions, playing surfaces or
grain do not match the attached photograph, the image is unusable no matter how
good the room looks, and this is the most likely failure.

Then reject and regenerate if:

  - any screen, cable or piece of studio equipment has appeared
  - the room looks styled, American, or like a furniture catalogue
  - the lighting is warm and golden rather than daylight
  - a person, a face or a pair of hands has appeared
  - the instrument is centred and isolated, which turns it back into a product
    shot
  - the colour has been graded into something cinematic

Whatever comes back is a wireframe placeholder. The real version of this image
is a photograph taken in the Budapest workshop or in a player's flat, and it
will be better than anything generated, because the point of the picture is
that the room is real.

RELATED DECISION

The product page currently has no hero image, on the grounds that the
instrument already appears in the order fork preview and two placeholder blocks
on one screen would be weight without information. A real photograph changes
that. If a hero is added, the two images need distinct jobs: the hero shows the
instrument situated, and the fork preview shows the object itself, which is
what someone choosing a wood actually needs to see.
